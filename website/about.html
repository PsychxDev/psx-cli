About PsX

A command-line project built around simplicity, freedom, and Linux.

What is PsX

PsX is a command-line toolkit designed to bring useful tools and everyday utilities together in one place.

The Idea

PsX started with a simple idea: make useful command-line tools easy to access without getting in the user's way.

License

PsX is released under an open-source license, allowing you to use, study, modify, and share the project.
